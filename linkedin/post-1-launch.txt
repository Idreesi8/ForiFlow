🚀 I spent 3 months building an AI credit-scoring prototype for Pakistani SME lenders.

The Problem:
• SME financing is only 6.06% of private-sector credit (SBP, 31 Dec 2024)
• Pakistan's MSME non-performing loan ratio was 15.0% in 2023 (ADB, 2024)
• Lenders need to explain automated credit decisions to borrowers and reviewers

The Solution — ForiFlow:
✅ Scores SMEs using alternative data (digital payments, officer-entered ECIB-style history)
✅ SHAP explainability for every decision — built with SBP review in mind (not certified)
✅ Early Warning System: monthly re-score, alert when the score drops more than 15 points
✅ One-command Docker deployment for bank demos

Built with: FastAPI ⚡ React ⚡ XGBoost ⚡ SHAP ⚡ Docker
