What does it take to build a bank-grade AI system as a solo developer?

Stack decisions I made:

⚡ FastAPI over Flask
Async by default, auto Swagger docs, Pydantic validation — perfect for ML APIs

⚡ React + Vite over vanilla JS
Component architecture, fast HMR, Tailwind for rapid UI

⚡ Docker over manual deployment
One command: docker-compose up -d
Bank IT teams love this.

⚡ SHAP over LIME
TreeExplainer is exact, not approximate. Regulators demand precision.

Biggest lesson?
Shipping > Perfection. Get the MVP in front of users, then iterate.
