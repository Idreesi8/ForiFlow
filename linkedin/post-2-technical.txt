Why did I choose XGBoost + Random Forest over a neural network for credit scoring?

3 reasons:

1️⃣ Interpretability > Black Box
Banks need to explain WHY a loan was rejected. SHAP works beautifully with tree-based models. Try explaining a neural network to SBP regulators 😅

2️⃣ Ensemble Robustness
XGBoost catches non-linear patterns. Random Forest reduces overfitting. Together they give stable, reliable scores.

3️⃣ Production Simplicity
Tree models are fast, lightweight, and easy to retrain. No GPU needed. Perfect for on-premise bank servers.

Result: 5-fold CV 0.7758 ± 0.0075, hold-out 0.7756 (n=32,581, 3 features, trained on a public/proxy dataset — not a real SME portfolio). 0.85+ remains a bank-data target, not a measured result.
