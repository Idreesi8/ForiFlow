Title: ForiFlow — SME Credit Intelligence Platform

Description:
ForiFlow scores unbanked Pakistani SMEs on alternative data (digital payments, ECIB, inventory) with an XGBoost + Random Forest ensemble, explains every decision with SHAP waterfalls for SBP audit files, and watches approved borrowers with an Early Warning System that flags a >15-point score drop. FastAPI, React 18, Docker. On-premise demo in one command.

Link: https://github.com/Idreesi8/foriflow

Skills tagged: FastAPI, React, XGBoost, SHAP, Docker
