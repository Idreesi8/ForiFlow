What does it take to build a bank-grade AI system as a solo developer?

Fewer frameworks. More boring decisions.

FastAPI over Flask because Pydantic ranges reject a bad loan amount before it touches the model. React over Vue because Recharts and the hiring market I am in already speak it. SQLite on the laptop, PostgreSQL when a bank asks — not a cloud bill on day one.

Docker was not optional. A credit head will not install Python 3.11. One compose file, nginx in front, /api on the same origin, CORS out of the demo path.

SHAP was the hard part. XGBoost 3 enables categorical splits by default; TreeExplainer does not. First-request latency was 49 seconds until I loaded the ensemble at startup. A “strong” applicant came back Rejected until I stopped treating credit-history length as repayment quality.

Lesson: the model is 30% of the work. The other 70% is making it operable for someone who does not care what a pickle file is.
