I spent 3 months building an AI system that could save Pakistani banks millions...

Not a slide deck. A running credit desk.

The brief I gave myself: score SMEs that have no collateral file, explain the decision in language an SBP auditor will accept, and warn the bank before the account goes bad.

The journey was messy in the useful ways. Public datasets are in USD; ForiFlow underwrites in PKR, so every feature had to be a ratio. SHAP and XGBoost 3 disagreed about categorical splits. The first “good borrower” I scored came back Rejected because I had encoded payment history the wrong way. Docker on a Windows laptop taught me more about PATH than I wanted to know.

What shipped: an XGBoost + Random Forest ensemble, a React officer dashboard, SHAP waterfalls on every file, and an Early Warning System that alerts on a drop of more than 15 points. One command — docker compose up — and a bank laptop is a demo.

If you work in credit, model risk, or fintech hiring, the repo is public.
